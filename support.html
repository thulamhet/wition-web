Support

A little help, so you can get back to creating.

Contact support

Email: wition.support@gmail.com

Include your Wition version and build number from Settings, your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; hide any personal details before sending it. Never send passwords or payment-card information.

A few quick answers

I purchased Pro, but it is still locked

Check that you are signed into the Apple Account used for the purchase and have an internet connection. Open Wition Settings or the purchase screen and select Restore Purchases. If the problem remains, contact support with the plan name and any error message.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Manage Subscriptions in the App Store and select Wition. You can also use Manage Subscriptions in Wition Settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I bought Lifetime while subscribed

Lifetime is a one-time purchase, but it does not cancel an existing subscription. Cancel the subscription separately through the App Store if you no longer want it to renew.

How do I request a refund?

Visit Apple’s Report a Problem website with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple manages refund requests.

Wition cannot import or save photos

Open iOS Settings, find Wition, and check photo-library access. You can open the app’s iOS settings through Photo Library access in Wition Settings. If access is limited, make sure the intended photo is included. Also check available device storage.

What does Clear Cache remove?

It removes disposable app cache, temporary share files, and cached network responses. It keeps dashboard photos, saved edits, and custom presets. Delete saved dashboard edits separately if you want to remove them.

Will my saved edits move to another device?

Saved dashboard edits and presets are stored locally. Keep exported copies of important results. Wition does not currently offer its own account-based cloud synchronization for edits.

How do I manage notifications or make a privacy request?

Change notification permissions in iOS Settings. For questions about personal information, contact the support address above and read the Privacy Policy. The current app does not have a separate Analytics or Crashlytics collection switch.
