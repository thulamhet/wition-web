Privacy Policy

Your photos. Your choices. A clear picture of how Wition handles information.

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Wition is a photo-editing app provided by Nguyễn Công Thư, based in Vietnam. This policy explains how the app and this website handle information.

PhotosUsage & diagnosticsPurchasesYour choicesContact

1. Photos and local editing data

Wition accesses photos you select or permit through iOS photo-library permissions. Editing is performed on your device. Saved dashboard edits, original image copies, editing adjustments, and custom presets are stored locally. Some editing tools analyze image regions on-device to apply an effect.

The current app does not upload your selected photos to a developer-operated photo-editing server. When you choose to export, share, or copy an image, the image is sent to the destination you select. That destination handles it under its own policies. Device backups and photo-library synchronization depend on your Apple settings.

2. Usage, diagnostics, and notifications

Wition uses Google Firebase services. Firebase Analytics receives app-usage events, such as interactions with editing tools and purchase flows. Crashlytics receives crash reports and technical diagnostics. Cloud Messaging uses installation identifiers and messaging tokens to deliver notifications. These SDKs may process device, app, installation, and network information according to their configuration.

We use this information to understand app usage, diagnose problems, and deliver notifications. Notification permission is managed through iOS. The current app does not provide a separate in-app switch for Analytics or Crashlytics collection.

For provider information, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Wition uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information, including product identifiers, verification results, expiration dates, and revocation information, to unlock and restore Pro access. Wition does not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase-related interaction events may also be recorded through Analytics.

4. Support correspondence

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, device details, and a description of the issue. We use it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or private photos unless you have chosen to share an example necessary to investigate the issue.

5. Retention and security

Local saved edits and presets remain until you remove them or remove the app’s local data. Exported photos, device backups, and copies shared elsewhere must be managed separately. Clearing temporary cache does not delete saved dashboard edits or presets.

Firebase information is retained under the relevant service policies and the retention settings configured for Wition. Wition’s Analytics retention settings are two months for event data and fourteen months for user data. The retention period for user identifiers resets on new user activity. These settings do not apply to standard aggregated reports; other Firebase services follow their own retention rules. Support correspondence is kept as long as necessary to resolve the support request. We use platform storage protections and service safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Change photo-library and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete saved edits from the dashboard and manage custom presets inside Wition.
  • Use Clear Cache to remove disposable app cache and temporary share files.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us to make a request. We may need enough information to identify the relevant records; pseudonymous SDK data may not be linked to an identifiable support requester.

7. International processing and younger users

Third-party providers may process technical information outside your country. Wition is available worldwide. Applicable protections and transfer arrangements depend on the provider and your location.

Wition is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Applicable local age and consent requirements may also apply. If you believe a child’s personal information has been collected contrary to these requirements, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. This website and policy updates

These pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising scripts, or contact forms. They are hosted through Vercel, whose infrastructure may process connection information when you visit. See Vercel’s Privacy Policy.

We may update this policy when practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Where required, we will provide notice or obtain consent for material changes.
